ABOUT AUTO-REBALANCER

This Dapp is a 100% free, decentralized liquidity management tool specifically designed for Aerodrome Slipstream (V3) pools on the Base Network. Below is a detailed breakdown of all available features.

V3 AUTO-REBALANCING

When the market price exits your defined Lower or Upper bounds, the system automatically pulls your liquidity. It then calculates the optimal 50/50 ratio for the new price, executes any necessary asset swaps, and remints a fresh, perfectly centered concentrated liquidity position.

AUTO-COMPOUND

Instead of just accumulating AERO rewards passively, the bot monitors your Pending Rewards. Once they hit your configured Min Cmpd threshold (e.g., 5 AERO), the tool automatically claims the AERO, swaps it for your pool's underlying tokens, and adds it back to your principal position, massively boosting your APY through continuous compounding.

SHIFT % (DYNAMIC RECENTERING)

A proactive rebalancing trigger. If you set a Shift % of 5%, the bot will rebalance your position if the price moves 5% away from your exact center point—even if it hasn't fallen entirely out of your safe bounds yet. This keeps your capital maximally active near the live price.

GAS JAR SYSTEM

Automated smart contracts cost gas. When enabled, the Gas Jar automatically diverts 2% of your harvested compound rewards and swaps them into ETH. This creates a self-sustaining loop where the bot constantly replenishes its own gas tank, allowing it to run indefinitely unattended without you needing to top off the wallet.

BOT MODE

For true automation, you cannot manually click "Approve" on every transaction via MetaMask. By entering a Burner Wallet Private Key, the app signs transactions silently in the background. The key is held strictly in temporary RAM and disappears the moment you close or refresh the tab. Always use a burner wallet with limited funds!

DIRECT SWAP & BACKGROUND MONITOR

The tool includes an integrated Direct Swap aggregator (via Odos API) for lightning-fast token trades with low slippage. The Monitor Toggle runs a continuous background loop every 30 seconds to fetch live pool data, updating the visualizer graph, pending fees, and instantly triggering automated actions exactly when needed.
